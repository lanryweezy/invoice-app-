# Midas (Growth & Conversion)

## 2026-08-26 - Frictionless Onboarding & Time-to-Value
**Learning:** Every additional click or form field during onboarding exponentially increases user drop-off. Competitors require lengthy account creation and KYC before a single invoice is sent, frustrating small business owners.
**Action:** Relentlessly audit the UI to minimize clicks. Never block the core action (generating an invoice) behind unnecessary authentication or setup walls. Allow users to experience the "Aha!" moment first, then prompt them to save/secure their session.

## 2026-08-26 - SEO & Discoverability (Organic Acquisition)
**Learning:** Organic search ("free invoice generator nigeria", "wht calculator") is the most cost-effective acquisition channel. Single Page Applications (SPAs) often fail at SEO if metadata isn't explicitly managed.
**Action:** Ensure all public-facing routes (marketing pages, template galleries, blogs, tool pages) include highly targeted <title> tags, meta descriptions, Open Graph (OG) images, and valid JSON-LD structured data to capture high-intent Google traffic.

## 2026-08-26 - Retention & Reactivation Triggers
**Learning:** A user sending their first invoice is just the beginning. Without proactive nudges, retention drops significantly. Users often don't realize the app can also track expenses and handle receipts.
**Action:** Implement non-intrusive re-engagement triggers. Use targeted Push Notifications or local reminders (e.g., "You haven't tracked an expense this week!" or "Did your client pay invoice #004?") for users who have been inactive for more than 7 days.

## 2026-08-26 - Competitor Exploitation in Copywriting
**Learning:** General software copy fails to convert. Users need to know *why* this app is better than Zoho, Paystack, or Wave specifically for their local context.
**Action:** Ensure all empty states, tooltips, and landing page copy aggressively highlight the app's unique moats: "True Offline Support", "Instant NIBSS Verification", "Zero Setup Needed", and "Built-in Stamp Duty & WHT".

## 2026-08-26 - Funnel Analytics Over Micro-Optimizations
**Learning:** Engineering perfect, performant code doesn't matter if users abandon the cart.
**Action:** Prioritize adding telemetry to business logic drop-off points (e.g., tracking when a user starts an invoice but closes the tab before generating the PDF). Use this data to continually simplify the user flow.
