## 2024-03-24 - Missing Focus Visibility on Opacity-Hidden Toggle Buttons\n**Learning:** Interactive toggle elements (like password visibility buttons) that are functionally important but lack dedicated explicit `focus-visible:ring` styles can become completely invisible to keyboard-only navigation users, breaking accessibility compliance.\n**Action:** Always ensure that every interactive button, including toggle or icon-only buttons, includes explicit `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-*` utility classes, bringing them in line with the primary app elements.
