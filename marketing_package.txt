### 3. FAQ Section (for rich snippets)
**Q: How much does manual bookkeeping actually cost a small business?**
A: Beyond the software or accountant fees, manual bookkeeping costs a business in "Admin Tax"—the hours wasted on reconciliation, the cashflow delays caused by forgotten invoices, and the lack of real-time visibility that prevents agile decision-making.

**Q: What is a Financial Operating System (FOS)?**
A: A Financial Operating System is an integrated tech stack that treats money movement as a seamless workflow. Instead of using disconnected tools (spreadsheets, word docs, bank apps), an FOS automates invoicing, embeds payment rails directly into documents, and auto-reconciles transactions.

**Q: How can I speed up my invoice collection process?**
A: Transition from sending static PDFs to using dynamic, automated invoices with embedded payment gateways (like Paystack or Flutterwave). Set clear upfront terms and rely on software to automatically follow up with clients.
