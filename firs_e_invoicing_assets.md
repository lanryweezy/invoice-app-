## FAQ (Snippet-Ready)
**What is FIRS e-invoicing?**
FIRS e-invoicing is a mandatory system rolling out in 2026 that requires Nigerian businesses to generate electronic invoices that are instantly validated and logged by the Federal Inland Revenue Service to ensure real-time tax compliance.

**Who must use FIRS e-invoicing in 2026?**
The mandate is expected to initially target larger enterprises and registered VAT-collecting businesses, with a phased rollout eventually requiring compliance from most registered SMEs and freelancers in Nigeria.

**How does the e-invoicing system affect VAT?**
The system automates and standardizes VAT reporting. When an e-invoice is generated, the 7.5% VAT (or applicable rate) is calculated, logged, and tracked centrally, eliminating manual end-of-year calculations and discrepancies.
