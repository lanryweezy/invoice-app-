*   **What is the FIRS e-invoicing mandate?** It's a regulatory requirement for businesses to issue electronic invoices validated by the FIRS system.

*   **Will I still be able to use Excel or Word for invoices?** No, manual invoices will not be compliant. You will need integrated software.
*   **How does this affect my VAT?** E-invoicing automates VAT tracking at the source of the transaction.
