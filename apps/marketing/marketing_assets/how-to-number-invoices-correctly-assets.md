## 3. FAQ Section (Google Snippet-Ready)
**Q: Can I start my first invoice with number 001?**
A: While legally permissible, starting at 001 is not recommended as it signals to clients that your business is brand new. Professional operators typically start their numbering sequence at 1001 or 1050 to project experience and reliability.

**Q: Should an invoice number have letters and numbers?**
A: Yes, using alphanumeric invoice numbers (e.g., INV-1001 or MTN-2024-01) is a best practice. The letters can act as client codes or project identifiers, making it easier for your finance team to organize and track payments.

**Q: What happens if I make a mistake on an invoice number?**
A: Never delete an invoice, as missing sequential numbers can trigger tax audit flags with the FIRS. Instead, issue a Credit Note to cancel the incorrect invoice, and generate a brand new, sequentially numbered invoice with the correct details.
