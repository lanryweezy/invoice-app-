## 3. FAQ Section

**Q: What is the Nigerian e-invoicing mandate?**
A: Starting in 2026, the FIRS and NRS will require businesses to issue electronic invoices that are validated in real-time through a centralized government portal to ensure immediate VAT tracking and tax compliance.

**Q: Can I still use Excel or Word for my invoices?**
A: No. Standard PDFs and Excel sheets won't be compliant because they lack the required digital authentication, QR codes, and real-time API validation demanded by the new mandate.

**Q: Who does the e-invoicing rule apply to?**
A: It applies to registered businesses, SMEs, agencies, and large corporations operating in Nigeria that issue B2B invoices and process VAT.

**Q: How do I ensure my invoices are compliant?**
A: The most effective method is transitioning to a modern financial operating system like InvoiceApp.ng, which automatically handles the necessary API validations, TIN checks, and QR code generation in the background.
