## FAQ
**Q: What is the FIRS e-invoicing mandate for 2026?**
A: The FIRS e-invoicing mandate is a regulatory shift requiring Nigerian businesses to issue electronic invoices that are validated by the government's tax system in real-time, aimed at ensuring VAT compliance and formalizing the economy.

**Q: Do I need to include 7.5% VAT on my invoices?**
A: Yes, if your business is registered for VAT and provides vatable goods or services in Nigeria, you must explicitly calculate and include the 7.5% VAT on your professional invoices.

**Q: Can I still use Excel for invoicing in Nigeria?**
A: While currently possible, using Excel is highly discouraged. It lacks automated tax compliance, payment gateway integrations, and will not meet the technical requirements for the upcoming 2026 e-invoicing mandate.
