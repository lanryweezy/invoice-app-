## 3. FAQ Section
**Q: What comes first, the purchase order or the invoice?**
A: The purchase order (PO) always comes first. The buyer issues the PO to request goods or services and confirm the budget. The seller issues the invoice later to request payment for those delivered goods.

**Q: Can I send an invoice without a purchase order?**
A: While you can, it's highly risky when dealing with corporate clients. Without a PO, there is no official proof that the buyer's company authorized the purchase, which often leads to unpaid invoices.

**Q: Is a purchase order a legally binding contract?**
A: Yes. Once a vendor accepts a purchase order from a buyer, it forms a legally binding contract detailing the goods, prices, and terms of delivery.
