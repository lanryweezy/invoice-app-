## 3. FAQ
**Q: What is the biggest mistake Nigerian SMEs make with invoicing?**
A: Relying on static Excel and PDF templates. This introduces friction, making it impossible to track invoice views or embed direct payment links.

**Q: Do I need to include VAT on my invoices?**
A: Yes, if your business meets the VAT registration threshold. Failing to clearly state the 7.5% VAT and your TIN will cause large corporate clients to reject your invoice.

**Q: How can I stop clients from delaying payments?**
A: Set clear payment terms (like Net-15 or Due on Receipt), implement automated follow-up reminders, and provide one-click payment options via Paystack or Flutterwave.
