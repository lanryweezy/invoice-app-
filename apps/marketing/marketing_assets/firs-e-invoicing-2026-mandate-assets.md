## 3. FAQ Section
**Q: What is the FIRS 2026 e-invoicing mandate?**
A: It is a forthcoming regulation by the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) requiring Nigerian businesses to generate and validate invoices electronically in real-time for tax and VAT compliance.

**Q: Can I still use Excel or Word for my invoices?**
A: By 2026, static Excel or Word invoices will not meet the real-time validation and embedded VAT requirements set by FIRS. You will need compliant invoicing software.

**Q: How does this affect my VAT calculations?**
A: VAT will need to be structurally integrated and validated at the point of invoice creation, eliminating the ability to manually reconcile or backdate tax data months later.
