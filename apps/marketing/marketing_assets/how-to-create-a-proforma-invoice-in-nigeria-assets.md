## FAQ Section
**What is the difference between a proforma invoice and a standard invoice in Nigeria?**
A proforma invoice is a preliminary bill sent to request commitment or an advance payment before work is done. A standard tax invoice is a legal demand for payment issued after goods are delivered or services rendered.

**Is a proforma invoice legally binding in Nigeria?**
No, a proforma invoice is a good-faith estimate and is not a legally binding contract or a finalized tax document until the terms are accepted and work commences.

**Do I add 7.5% VAT to a proforma invoice?**
You should indicate the expected VAT amount on a proforma invoice so the client knows the final cost, but the actual tax liability to FIRS is only triggered when the final tax invoice is issued.

**Can I use a proforma invoice for customs clearance in Nigeria?**
Yes, a proforma invoice is strictly required by the Nigerian Customs Service and the CBN to process a Form M and allocate foreign exchange for imports.
