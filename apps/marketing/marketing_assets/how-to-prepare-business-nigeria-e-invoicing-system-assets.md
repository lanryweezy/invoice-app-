## 3. FAQ Section
**Q: When is the deadline for e-invoicing in Nigeria?**
A: Large businesses (>₦5B) are currently under enforcement. Medium businesses (₦1B–₦5B) must comply by July 1, 2026. Small businesses (<₦1B) have until July 1, 2027, but early adoption is heavily encouraged to avoid operational disruption.

**Q: Can I still send invoices as standard PDFs via email?**
A: No. Under the new mandate, invoices must be generated in the Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 UBL format, submitted to the NRS for real-time validation, and stamped with a unique QR code before they are legally valid to send.

**Q: What happens if my business ignores the mandate?**
A: Non-compliance carries severe penalties, starting at ₦200,000 plus 100% of the tax due. Furthermore, your B2B clients will not accept unvalidated invoices because they won't be able to claim VAT inputs.
