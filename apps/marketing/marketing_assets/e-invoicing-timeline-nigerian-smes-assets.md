## FAQ Section
**Q: When does the FIRS e-invoicing mandate take full effect?**
A: The FIRS e-invoicing system is scheduled for full nationwide rollout in 2026, making electronic validation mandatory for B2B transactions.

**Q: Can I still use Excel or Word for my invoices after 2026?**
A: No. Invoices will be processed and validated through FIRS-approved software to receive a unique digital signature or QR code. Unvalidated invoices will not be accepted for tax deductions.

**Q: How should my small business prepare for e-invoicing today?**
A: Start by auditing your current invoicing process, collecting client Tax Identification Numbers (TINs), and transitioning from manual spreadsheets to a compliant cloud-based invoicing platform like InvoiceApp.ng.
