## 3. FAQ Section
**Q: Can I just send my account number to a client on WhatsApp?**
A: While common in Nigeria, sending an account number on WhatsApp lacks professionalism and causes payment delays, especially with corporate clients who need formal invoices for their accounting and tax compliance.

**Q: How can I accept international payments as a Nigerian freelancer?**
A: You can accept international payments by using an invoicing platform that integrates with global payment gateways. InvoiceApp.ng allows you to embed multi-currency payment links directly into your invoices.

**Q: Should I include VAT on my freelance invoices in Nigeria?**
A: If you are a registered business entity providing vatable services, you should include 7.5% VAT. Using an automated platform like InvoiceApp.ng ensures your tax calculations are accurate and FIRS-compliant.

**Q: How do I handle clients who refuse to pay on time?**
A: The best approach is proactive automation. Instead of manual follow-ups, use an invoicing software to send automated email reminders before, on, and after the due date to enforce professional boundaries without emotional strain.
