## 3. FAQ Section
**Q: Can I just send a WhatsApp message with the total cost?**
A: No. A WhatsApp message isn't a legally binding document and leaves room for scope creep. A professional invoice clearly defines the deliverables, protecting you from unpaid alterations.

**Q: How do I handle 70% deposits and 30% final payments?**
A: Use an automated invoicing system like InvoiceApp.ng to log the initial deposit against the total bill. The system will automatically track the outstanding balance for the final payment.

**Q: What is a Proforma Invoice and do fashion brands need it?**
A: A Proforma Invoice is a confirmed estimate sent before work begins. It is essential for fashion brands to secure advance payments (mobilization) before purchasing fabric or starting production.

**Q: Are paper receipts still acceptable for high-end fashion?**
A: While legal, paper receipts create a cognitive dissonance for premium clients. If you charge premium prices, your financial documentation should reflect that same level of professionalism.
