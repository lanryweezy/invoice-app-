A: It is a system where invoice data is transmitted digitally and in real-time to the FIRS for instant validation and VAT capturing, replacing manual paper or PDF invoices.

**Q: When does FIRS e-invoicing become mandatory in Nigeria?**
A: The rollout is targeted for 2026, though larger corporations may begin phased implementations earlier.
