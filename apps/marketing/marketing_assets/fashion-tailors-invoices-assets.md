## 3. FAQ
**Q: How much should I charge as a deposit for custom tailoring in Nigeria?**
A: Most premium fashion brands in Nigeria charge a 60% to 70% non-refundable deposit upfront to cover the cost of fabric and initial labor, with the final balance due strictly before delivery or pickup.

**Q: Should I separate fabric costs from workmanship on the invoice?**
A: Yes. Itemizing fabric, embellishments, and workmanship increases transparency, justifies your premium pricing, and reduces haggling from clients.

**Q: Can I use InvoiceApp to track partial payments for my fashion business?**
A: Absolutely. InvoiceApp allows you to issue a master invoice, record the initial deposit, and automatically track and send reminders for the outstanding balance.
