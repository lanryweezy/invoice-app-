## 3. FAQ Section (Google Snippet-Ready)

**Q: What is FIRS e-invoicing?**
A: FIRS e-invoicing is a mandatory digital system being rolled out by the Federal Inland Revenue Service in Nigeria by 2026. It requires businesses to generate invoices through compliant software that validates transaction data in real-time with the FIRS, often issuing a unique QR code to prove authenticity.

**Q: Who needs to use FIRS e-invoicing in 2026?**
A: Eventually, all registered businesses in Nigeria, including SMEs, startups, and freelancers, will be required to comply with the e-invoicing mandate to issue legally recognized B2B and B2C invoices and process VAT.

**Q: Can I still use Excel or Word to create invoices?**
A: No. Once the mandate is fully enforced, static PDFs created in Excel or Word cannot perform the real-time API validation required by the FIRS. Businesses will need to switch to FIRS-compliant invoicing software.
