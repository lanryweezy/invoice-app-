## 3. FAQ Section
**Q: Do I always have to charge 7.5% VAT on my invoices?**
A: If you are dealing with VAT-able goods and services in Nigeria, you are legally required to include the 7.5% VAT on your tax invoices and remit it to the FIRS.

**Q: Can a corporate client refuse to pay me if my invoice is wrong?**
A: Yes. Enterprise clients cannot claim input VAT without a fully compliant tax invoice containing your TIN. They will reject non-compliant invoices to protect themselves from FIRS penalties.

**Q: Does a generic "Proforma Invoice" count as a Tax Invoice?**
A: No. FIRS requires the document to specifically state "Tax Invoice" and include specific identifiers like your TIN and the buyer's details to be valid for tax purposes.
