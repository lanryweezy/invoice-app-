## 3. FAQ Section
**Q: How do I invoice for a construction milestone in Nigeria?**
A: To invoice for a milestone, clearly detail the project phase, percentage of completion, specific materials and labor used, and automatically calculate applicable taxes like 7.5% VAT and Withholding Tax. Always include a unique invoice number and clear payment terms.

**Q: What is retainage in construction invoicing?**
A: Retainage is a portion of the contract price (typically 5% to 10%) withheld by the client until the project reaches practical completion. Your invoice should clearly state the gross amount, minus the retainage, to show the exact net amount due for that milestone.

**Q: How will the FIRS 2026 e-invoicing mandate affect construction businesses?**
A: Construction firms dealing with large B2B transactions will need to use FIRS-compliant software that generates cryptographically verifiable electronic invoices, ensuring real-time reporting of VAT and transaction data.

**Q: Can I use Excel for construction invoicing?**
A: While possible, using Excel for construction invoicing is highly error-prone. It lacks automated tax compliance, milestone tracking, and secure audit trails, which often leads to delayed payments from corporate clients.
