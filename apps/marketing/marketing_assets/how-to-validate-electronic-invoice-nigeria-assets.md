## FAQ
**Q: What happens if I pay an unvalidated invoice?**
A: You will not be able to claim the 7.5% VAT input tax credit, meaning you effectively lose that money, and you may face audit discrepancies with the FIRS.

**Q: How do I scan a FIRS e-invoice QR code?**
A: You can use a standard smartphone camera or an official NRS validation application to scan the QR code. It will direct you to a secure FIRS portal confirming the transaction details.

**Q: Can I validate an invoice without a QR code?**
A: Yes. You can log into the FIRS/NRS portal and manually enter the Invoice Reference Number (IRN) found on the document to verify its status.
