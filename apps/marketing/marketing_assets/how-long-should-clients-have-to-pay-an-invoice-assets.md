## 3. FAQ Section (For Google Snippets)

**What is the standard payment term for an invoice?**
Globally, Net 30 (payment due in 30 days) is common for enterprise B2B. However, for SMEs, freelancers, and agencies, "Due on Receipt" or "Net 15" is strongly recommended to maintain healthy cashflow.

**Can I charge a late fee for unpaid invoices in Nigeria?**
Yes, if it is explicitly stated in your contract and clearly visible on your invoice. A standard 2% to 5% monthly late fee acts as a strong deterrent against delayed payments.

**How do I politely ask for an overdue payment?**
Remove the human emotion by using automated follow-up software. A simple, professional system-generated email stating the invoice is overdue is highly effective and preserves the client relationship.
