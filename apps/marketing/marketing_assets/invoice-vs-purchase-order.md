## FAQ Section
**Q: Can I send an invoice without a Purchase Order?**
A: While possible for small clients, large corporate entities operate on a "No PO, No Pay" policy. Always secure a signed Purchase Order before commencing work to guarantee payment.

**Q: Who issues the Purchase Order?**
A: The buyer (your client) issues the Purchase Order to authorize the purchase and confirm budget availability.

**Q: Does an invoice need to match the Purchase Order exactly?**
A: Yes. Any discrepancy in line items, descriptions, or amounts between your invoice and the client's PO will likely cause their accounts payable department to reject the invoice, delaying your payment.
