## 3. FAQ Section
- **Q: Can I use Microsoft Word or Excel to create invoices in Nigeria?**
  A: Yes, many businesses start with Word or Excel templates. However, they lack automation, are prone to human error, and do not provide real-time cash flow insights. As your business scales, migrating to dedicated invoicing software is recommended.
- **Q: What must be included on a Nigerian invoice?**
  A: A valid Nigerian invoice must include your business details (and TIN if registered), client details, invoice number, date, line items with prices, total amount, any applicable VAT, and clear payment instructions.
- **Q: Are PDF invoices legally binding in Nigeria?**
  A: Yes, a digitally delivered PDF invoice is generally accepted as a legal request for payment, provided it contains accurate transaction details and complies with standard commercial practices.
- **Q: How will the 2026 FIRS e-invoicing mandate affect my templates?**
  A: By 2026, manual templates (Word/Excel) will likely be insufficient for B2B transactions, as FIRS will require real-time validation and generation of unique identifiers (like QR codes) that manual templates cannot support natively.
