## FAQ Section
**Q: Can I still use Excel or Word for invoices in Nigeria?**
A: While currently possible, it is operationally inefficient, prone to errors, and looks unprofessional. More importantly, when FIRS mandates e-invoicing in 2026, manual PDFs and Excel sheets will no longer be compliant.

**Q: Do I always have to include VAT on my invoice?**
A: If your business is registered for VAT and your goods/services are not exempt, you are legally required to charge 7.5% VAT and remit it to FIRS.

**Q: What is the FIRS E-Invoicing mandate of 2026?**
A: It is an upcoming regulation requiring businesses to issue electronic invoices that are validated in real-time by the FIRS system, ensuring tax compliance and reducing the VAT gap.

**Q: How can I ensure clients pay my invoices on time?**
A: Use clear, granular line items, specify exact due dates (avoid "Due on Receipt"), embed payment links for zero-friction transactions, and automate professional follow-ups.
