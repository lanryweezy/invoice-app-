## 3. FAQ Section
**Q: Can I put a Paystack link in a PDF invoice?**
A: You can hyperlink text in a PDF, but it's clunky and doesn't auto-reconcile. It's better to use a cloud invoicing platform like InvoiceApp.ng that hosts a dynamic invoice with a native payment button.

**Q: Who pays the Paystack transaction fee?**
A: By default, the merchant (you) pays the fee. However, many invoicing platforms allow you to configure it so the customer bears the transaction fee.

**Q: Do I need to be a registered business to use Paystack?**
A: Paystack offers "Starter Businesses" for unregistered entities, but with limits. For full functionality, having a registered business (CAC) is recommended.

**Q: How does this help with reconciliation?**
A: When a client pays via an integrated payment button, the invoicing software automatically updates the invoice status to "Paid," eliminating the need to manually check your bank statement.
