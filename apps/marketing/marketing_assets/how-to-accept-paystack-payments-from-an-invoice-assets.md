A: No. When they click the payment link, they simply enter their card details, use USSD, or make a direct bank transfer via the Paystack gateway.

A: You only pay the standard transaction fee charged by Paystack (typically 1.5% + ₦100 for local transactions). InvoiceApp does not charge an additional gateway fee.

**Q: How does this help with FIRS compliance?**
