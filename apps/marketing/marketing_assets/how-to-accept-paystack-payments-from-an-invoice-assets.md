**Q: Can I put a Paystack link in a PDF invoice?**
A: You can hyperlink text in a PDF, but it's clunky and doesn't auto-reconcile. It's better to use a cloud invoicing platform like InvoiceApp.ng that hosts a dynamic invoice with a native payment button.

**Q: Who pays the Paystack transaction fee?**
A: By default, the merchant (you) pays the fee. However, many invoicing platforms allow you to configure it so the customer bears the transaction fee.

**Q: Do I need to be a registered business to use Paystack?**
A: Paystack offers "Starter Businesses" for unregistered entities, but with limits. For full functionality, having a registered business (CAC) is recommended.

## FAQ
**Q: Do my clients need a Paystack account to pay my invoice?**
A: No. When they click the payment link, they simply enter their card details, use USSD, or make a direct bank transfer via the Paystack gateway.

**Q: Are there extra fees for adding a payment link?**
A: You only pay the standard transaction fee charged by Paystack (typically 1.5% + ₦100 for local transactions). InvoiceApp does not charge an additional gateway fee.

**Q: How does this help with FIRS compliance?**
A: Digital payment records combined with automated VAT calculation create a clean, auditable trail that prepares your business for the 2026 e-invoicing mandates.

**Q: Can international clients pay through these links?**
A: Yes. Paystack supports international card payments, automatically settling in your local account, which removes the friction of complex wire transfers.
