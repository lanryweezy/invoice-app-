## 3. FAQ Section
**Q: Can I legally use Excel for invoicing in Nigeria?**
A: Yes, you can legally use Excel to create invoices, provided they contain all required information (Business Name, TIN, VAT, Date, Invoice Number). However, with the upcoming 2026 FIRS e-invoicing mandate, manual spreadsheets will not be able to generate the required digital validation, making them obsolete for B2B transactions.

**Q: Why do corporate clients prefer automated invoices over Excel PDFs?**
A: Corporate procurement teams require strict compliance. Automated invoices ensure perfect tax calculations, verifiable sequential numbering, and integrated payment links. Excel invoices are prone to human error, which triggers manual reviews and delays your payment.

**Q: What is the best alternative to Excel for invoicing in Nigeria?**
A: Dedicated financial operating systems like InvoiceApp.ng are the best alternatives. They automate formatting, VAT calculations, client tracking, and payment collection, saving you hours of administrative work each month.

**Q: How much time does manual invoicing actually waste?**
A: On average, creating, verifying, and sending an Excel invoice takes 15-20 minutes. If you send 20 invoices a month, that's over 6 hours of purely administrative work that could be automated in seconds using professional invoicing software.
