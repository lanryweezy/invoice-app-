## 3. FAQ Section

**Q: Do I always have to charge 7.5% VAT on my invoices?**
A: According to the FIRS, only businesses with an annual turnover of ₦25 million and above are mandated to charge and remit VAT. However, certain corporate clients may require VAT documentation regardless, so it's essential to understand your tax obligations.

**Q: Can I just write my personal bank account on a business invoice?**
A: It is highly discouraged. Using a personal account for a business invoice looks unprofessional and can cause corporate clients to flag the transaction for compliance reasons. Always use a corporate bank account that matches your registered business name.

**Q: What is a Proforma Invoice, and is it the same as a standard invoice?**
A: No. A proforma invoice is a preliminary bill sent to a buyer in advance of a shipment or delivery of goods. It acts as an estimate. A standard invoice is the official request for payment after goods or services have been delivered.

**Q: Do I need to include my BVN on the invoice?**
A: Absolutely not. Never include your Bank Verification Number (BVN) on a public document like an invoice. Only your Tax Identification Number (TIN) is required for corporate billing.
