## FAQ
**Q: Can I automate invoices for clients who pay via bank transfer?**
A: Yes. With InvoiceApp.ng, the automated invoice includes an embedded Paystack or Flutterwave link that allows clients to pay instantly via dedicated virtual bank accounts, cards, or USSD.

**Q: What if the client's retainer amount changes slightly every month?**
A: You can set up a recurring invoice as a draft. The system generates it automatically, but you can review and adjust any variable line items (like ad spend or extra hours) before clicking send.

**Q: Does InvoiceApp handle the 7.5% VAT on recurring invoices?**
A: Absolutely. Once you configure the tax settings on the recurring profile, VAT is automatically calculated and applied to every generated invoice, ensuring total compliance.
