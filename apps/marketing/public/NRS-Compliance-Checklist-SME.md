# NRS E-Invoicing Compliance Checklist for SMEs

Ensure your business is ready for the July 1, 2026 NRS mandatory go-live date.

---

## 📋 The 5-Step Compliance Checklist

### 1. Business Readiness
- [ ] **Verify TIN:** Confirm your business Tax Identification Number (TIN) is active and correct with the NRS.
- [ ] **Determine Turnover Band:** Identify which turnover threshold your business falls under (>₦5B, ₦1B-₦5B, or <₦1B) to know your exact compliance deadline.

### 2. Technical Infrastructure
- [ ] **Audit Current System:** If you use Word/Excel, prepare to migrate to a digital invoicing platform.
- [ ] **Select Accredited APP:** Choose an NRS-accredited Access Point Provider (APP) or System Integrator.
- [ ] **Test Workflow:** Ensure your invoicing system automatically generates the mandatory fields (TIN, VAT codes, Invoice Reference, Digital Signature).

### 3. Data Integrity
- [ ] **Client Database Update:** Collect and verify TINs for all your B2B corporate clients.
- [ ] **Product Catalog:** Ensure all products/services are correctly categorized with the NRS-mandated quantity and tax codes.

### 4. Process Automation
- [ ] **Digital Signing:** Ensure your invoicing platform supports cryptographic digital signing as required for NRS validation.
- [ ] **Real-Time Transmission:** Configure your system to transmit invoice data to the NRS Merchant Buyer Solution (MBS) portal at the point of issuance.

### 5. Final Compliance Review
- [ ] **Test Transaction:** Run a pilot e-invoice through the system to ensure it is correctly validated and logged in the central NRS ledger.
- [ ] **Audit Trail:** Confirm your dashboard keeps a secure, searchable log of all issued e-invoices for tax reporting purposes.

---

*This checklist is provided by InvoiceApp.ng to help Nigerian SMEs navigate the e-invoicing transition. For official regulatory updates, always consult the NRS Merchant Buyer Solution (MBS) portal.*
