**A:** The FIRS e-invoicing mandate requires all Nigerian businesses to issue electronic invoices that are validated in real-time by the Nigeria Revenue System (NRS) before being sent to clients.

**A:** The full nationwide rollout for the FIRS e-invoicing mandate is scheduled for 2026.
