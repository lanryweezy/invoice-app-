## 3. FAQ Section

**Q: What exactly is the 2026 FIRS e-invoicing mandate?**
A: By 2026, the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) requires businesses to transmit transaction data in real-time when issuing invoices. The system will validate the invoice and generate a QR code, making it legally compliant for tax purposes.

**Q: Can I still use Excel or Word templates for my invoices?**
A: No. Manual templates cannot generate the required real-time XML data or FIRS-validated QR codes. You will need compliant invoicing software to issue valid invoices.

**Q: What happens if I don't use the e-invoicing system?**
A: Your invoices will be considered legally invalid by the FIRS. This means your B2B clients cannot claim those expenses as tax deductions, which will likely lead them to stop doing business with you until you are compliant.

**Q: How does InvoiceApp.ng help with this?**
A: InvoiceApp.ng automates the entire process. It structures your invoice data correctly, calculates VAT automatically, and embeds payment links. It is being built to integrate seamlessly with the FIRS API, making compliance frictionless.
