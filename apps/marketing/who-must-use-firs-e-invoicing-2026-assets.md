## FAQ
* **Does the FIRS e-invoicing mandate apply to freelancers?**
  Technically, if you bill below ₦25 million annually, you are currently exempt from charging VAT. However, if your clients are larger corporations, they may require you to submit FIRS-compliant e-invoices for their own tax purposes, meaning market forces will likely require you to adapt.
* **What happens if I don’t use e-invoicing in 2026?**
  If you are mandated to use it and fail to do so, your invoices will lack the necessary FIRS cryptographic verification and QR codes. Consequently, corporate clients won't be able to process them, leading to severely delayed or rejected payments, alongside potential regulatory fines.
* **Can I still use Excel or Word for my invoices?**
  No. Excel and Word cannot dynamically validate TINs or automatically sync transaction data with FIRS servers in real time, both of which are required under the new MBS framework.
