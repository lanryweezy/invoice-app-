# FAQ
**Q: Can I use my local Naira account to receive international payments?**
A: While some gateways handle the conversion, it's highly recommended to use virtual USD accounts or specialized payment links to avoid poor exchange rates and high hidden fees.

**Q: Do international clients trust Nigerian payment gateways?**
A: Yes, if presented professionally. Using platforms like Paystack (which is backed by Stripe) provides a secure, familiar checkout experience for Western clients.

**Q: What currency should I use on my invoice?**
A: Always invoice in the client's preferred currency (usually USD, GBP, or EUR) to reduce their cognitive load and make the payment process seamless.
