<h2>What is a Proforma Invoice?</h2>
<p>A proforma invoice is a preliminary bill of sale sent to buyers in advance of a shipment or delivery of goods and services. Think of it as a “good faith” agreement. It outlines the exact deliverables, costs, and terms of the transaction before the final work is executed.</p>

<p>Unlike a final tax invoice, a proforma invoice is <em>not</em> a demand for payment that gets recorded as accounts receivable in a general ledger. It is an operational document designed to secure commitment, facilitate advance payments, or clear customs for physical goods.</p>

<h2>Proforma Invoice vs. Quotation vs. Final Invoice</h2>
<p>To run a tight back-office, you must stop treating these documents interchangeably:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Quotation:</strong> An initial estimate. Prices might fluctuate. It says, <em>"Here is what this might cost."</em></li>
  <li><strong>Proforma Invoice:</strong> A confirmed estimate. Prices are fixed unless scope changes. It says, <em>"Here is exactly what this will cost, please commit so we can begin."</em></li>
  <li><strong>Final Invoice:</strong> A legal demand for payment. The service is done (or milestones reached). It says, <em>"You owe us this money now."</em></li>
</ul>

<h2>Why Nigerian Businesses Need Proforma Invoices</h2>
<p>In Nigeria’s fast-moving, high-friction commerce environment, proforma invoices serve specific, critical functions:</p>

<h3>1. Securing Mobilization and Advance Payments</h3>
<p>Whether you run an agency in Yaba or a construction firm in Abuja, starting work without a mobilization fee is a cashflow death trap. Corporate clients cannot disburse funds against a mere "quote" or a final "invoice" for incomplete work. A proforma invoice provides the exact financial documentation procurement teams need to authorize an advance payment without violating internal controls.</p>

<h3>2. International Trade and Customs (Form M)</h3>
<p>If you are importing goods into Nigeria, the Nigerian Customs Service and your bank will require a proforma invoice to open a Letter of Credit or process a Form M. The Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) relies on this document to track FX allocation for imports. A poorly formatted proforma invoice here means your goods are stuck at Apapa port.</p>

<h3>3. VAT and FIRS Compliance (The 2026 Shift)</h3>
<p>As the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) rolls out mandatory e-invoicing for 2026, the timeline of when a tax invoice is generated becomes legally critical. If you issue a final invoice to request an advance payment, FIRS assumes the transaction is complete, making you immediately liable for the 7.5% VAT—even if the client hasn't paid you yet! Using a proforma invoice delays the tax liability until the final invoice is officially issued upon job completion.</p>

<h2>What Must Be Included in a Proforma Invoice?</h2>
<p>To prevent your document from being rejected by sharp procurement teams, ensure it includes:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>The Word "Proforma":</strong> It must clearly say "Proforma Invoice" at the top. Do not leave room for ambiguity.</li>
  <li><strong>Issue Date and Expiry Date:</strong> Because inflation and FX rates in Nigeria can shift rapidly, clearly state how long the pricing is valid (e.g., "Valid for 14 days").</li>
  <li><strong>Unique Reference Number:</strong> Use a logical numbering system (e.g., PRO-2024-001) separate from your final tax invoice numbers.</li>
  <li><strong>Buyer and Seller Details:</strong> Full company names, addresses, and TINs (Tax Identification Numbers).</li>
  <li><strong>Detailed Line Items:</strong> Specific descriptions, quantities, unit prices, and total amounts.</li>
  <li><strong>Terms of Sale and Payment:</strong> Explicitly state the required advance payment percentage (e.g., "70% upfront, 30% on completion").</li>
  <li><strong>Bank Details:</strong> Clearly list your corporate Naira or USD account details to eliminate back-and-forth WhatsApp messages.</li>
</ul>
