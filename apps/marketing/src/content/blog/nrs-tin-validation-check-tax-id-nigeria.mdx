<p>Your Tax Identification Number (TIN) is the single most important piece of data for NRS e-invoicing. If your TIN is invalid, expired, or incorrectly formatted, every invoice you generate will be rejected by the NRS system. Here's how to check and fix it before it costs you money.</p>

<h2>What Is a TIN and Why Does It Matter?</h2>
<p>A TIN is an 11-digit unique identifier issued by the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS), now under the Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS). It is required on every tax invoice issued by a registered business in Nigeria.</p>
<p>Without a valid TIN:</p>
<ul>
    <li>Your invoices will fail NRS validation</li>
    <li>You cannot claim input VAT credits</li>
    <li>Your Tax Clearance Certificate may be suspended</li>
    <li>Clients will refuse to do business with you</li>
</ul>

<h3>2. Check via the FIRS e-Portal</h3>

<h3>3. Visit a Tax Office</h3>
<p>If you suspect your TIN is inactive or incorrect, visit the nearest FIRS tax office with your CAC certificate and government-issued ID.</p>

<h2>Common TIN Errors That Get Invoices Rejected</h2>

<h3>Error 1: Wrong Number of Digits</h3>
<p>A valid Nigerian TIN is exactly 11 digits. Not 10, not 12. If you enter fewer or more digits, the NRS system will reject it immediately.</p>

<h3>Error 2: Using a Personal TIN for Business</h3>
<p>Individual TINs and business TINs are different. If you registered as a sole proprietor, your personal TIN may work for small transactions, but B2B invoices require the business TIN linked to your CAC registration.</p>

<h3>Error 3: Inactive or Suspended TIN</h3>
<p>A TIN can become inactive if:</p>
<ul>
    <li>You haven't filed returns in over 12 months</li>
    <li>Your business registration has lapsed</li>
    <li>There's a compliance hold on your account</li>
</ul>
<p>To reactivate, file your outstanding returns and clear any penalties.</p>

<h3>Error 4: TIN Not Linked to CAC Number</h3>
<p>The NRS cross-references your TIN with your CAC registration number. If they don't match in the system, your invoices will be flagged as non-compliant. Visit FIRS to link them.</p>

<h2>What Happens If Your TIN Fails Validation?</h2>
<p>If you issue an invoice with an invalid TIN:</p>
<ul>
    <li>The invoice is marked as "Failed" in the NRS system</li>
    <li>The buyer cannot claim input VAT on the transaction</li>
    <li>Both parties face compliance penalties</li>
    <li>Your compliance score drops, affecting future transactions</li>
</ul>
