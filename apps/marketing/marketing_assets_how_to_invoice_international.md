## 3. FAQ Section
**Q: Can I use my local Nigerian bank account to receive international payments?**
A: Yes, via SWIFT, but it often involves high intermediary fees, poor exchange rates, and delays. Using virtual foreign accounts is the modern standard.

**Q: Do I need to charge VAT on international invoices from Nigeria?**
A: Generally, exported services are zero-rated for VAT in Nigeria, but you should always consult a tax professional to ensure compliance.

**Q: What is the best currency to invoice international clients?**
A: Bill in the client's local currency or a standard hard currency (like USD) to reduce friction for them and protect your revenue from local currency devaluation.
