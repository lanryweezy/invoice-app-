### FAQ

**What is the hidden cost of manual bookkeeping?**
The hidden cost includes wasted administrative time, increased likelihood of human error, delayed payments (slower Cash Velocity), and the lost opportunity cost of capital tied up in unpaid invoices.

**How does automating invoicing improve cashflow?**
Automating invoicing removes friction for the client. By embedding direct payment links (like Paystack or Flutterwave) into the invoice and setting up automated reminders, clients pay faster, drastically reducing the cash conversion cycle.

**Why shouldn't I just use Excel or Word for invoices?**
Excel and Word create static documents that require manual data entry and separate banking reconciliation. They also look less professional, signaling to clients that your business is informal, which often leads to delayed payments.
