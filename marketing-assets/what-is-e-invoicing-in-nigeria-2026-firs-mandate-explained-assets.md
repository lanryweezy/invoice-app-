# 3. FAQ Section
- **What is an e-invoice in Nigeria?**
  An e-invoice is a structured digital file that is generated, transmitted, and validated by the FIRS in real-time, replacing manual PDFs and paper receipts.
- **When does e-invoicing become mandatory in Nigeria?**
  The FIRS is rolling out the mandatory e-invoicing framework (NRS) starting in 2026.
- **Do small businesses have to use e-invoicing?**
  Yes, the mandate will eventually cover all businesses registered for VAT, meaning SMEs must upgrade from manual Excel invoicing to compliant software.
