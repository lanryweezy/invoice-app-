## 3. FAQ Section
**Q: Why are manual invoices bad for business?**
A: Manual invoices (like Excel or Word templates) are prone to calculation errors, lack institutional authority, and make tracking late payments nearly impossible, which increases your Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) and harms cash flow.

**Q: How does a financial operating system help?**
A: A financial OS automates the tedious parts of bookkeeping—like VAT calculation, invoice numbering, and tracking payment status—freeing founders to focus on growth instead of administration.

**Q: Will manual invoicing be allowed in Nigeria in the future?**
A: With the upcoming FIRS 2026 e-invoicing mandate, Nigerian businesses will be required to use compliant electronic systems for B2B and B2G transactions, meaning manual PDFs and spreadsheets will face regulatory hurdles.
